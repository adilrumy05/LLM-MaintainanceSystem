Animation Spike Review

Twelve technician questions were put through a pipeline that turns manual passages into a step-by-step schematic animation. Two AI models were tried on each. This sheet shows every result so that people who know the equipment can judge whether the animations are correct and useful.

Check it against the manualEach line under a drawing is the manual's own wording, with its page number. Open that page and compare: steps, order, values, wiring.
Look for what is missingThe checks can catch a wrong arrow or wire. They cannot know about a warning the model never picked up. That part needs you.
Say if it would helpThe drawings are schematic: shapes and positions are not from the manual. Judge whether a technician would understand the job faster with this than with text alone.
